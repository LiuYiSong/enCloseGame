Privacy & Support

enClose · Last updated: August 25, 2026

This page explains how enClose handles information and how you can reach us for support. The game is designed to work with minimal personal data.

Information We Collect

  • Gameplay data on your device. Settings, high scores, and similar preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional platform features. If you use Game Center or similar services, Apple (or the platform provider) may process account and leaderboard data under their own privacy policies.
  • What we do not sell. We do not sell your personal information.

How Information Is Used

Local data is used only to run the game (for example restoring your settings and best score). We do not use your data for third-party advertising networks in the current version.

Children

enClose is a casual arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

Third-Party Services

The app may open links to the App Store (for ratings) or this support page in your system browser. Those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Support

Questions, feedback, or privacy requests: support@example.com

Changes

We may update this page from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
