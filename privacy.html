Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

enClose respects your privacy.

Data Collection

enClose does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, or developer-operated account systems.

Gameplay preferences and settings that need to be remembered by the app are stored locally on your device.

Game Center

enClose may use Apple Game Center for features such as leaderboards.

Game Center information is processed by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy practices. enClose does not independently collect, store, or maintain your Game Center account information.

Any Game Center data made available through Apple's services is used only as necessary to provide Game Center features within the app.

Permissions

enClose does not request access to sensitive device information or features such as your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Children's Privacy

enClose does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because enClose does not collect personal information, no personal user data is retained by the developer.

Third-Party Services

Other than Apple services required for platform functionality, such as Game Center, enClose does not use third-party services that collect user information on behalf of the developer.

Data Retention and Deletion

enClose does not maintain a server-side user database and does not retain personal information.

Local app preferences can be removed by deleting the app from your device.

Information associated with Apple Game Center is managed by Apple and is subject to Apple's policies and account controls.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change.

If enClose introduces functionality in the future that changes how user information is handled, this Privacy Policy and the corresponding App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.
