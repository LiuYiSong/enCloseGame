Privacy & Support

enClose · Last updated: August 25, 2026

enClose is a casual arcade game. We designed it to work without collecting personal information and without requesting special device permissions.

Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not include analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or account systems operated by us.

Any gameplay preferences or scores that the app remembers stay on your device only. That information is not sent to our servers (we do not operate app backends for this purpose).

Permissions

The current version of enClose does not request sensitive device permissions (for example camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library access).

Links Outside the App

From Settings, you may open:

  • this Privacy & Support page in your system browser; and
  • the App Store rating page for the app (or a placeholder store page during testing).

Those destinations are provided by Apple or GitHub Pages hosting and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Opening them does not give us access to your personal data.

Children

Because we do not collect personal information, we also do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
