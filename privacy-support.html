Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

Loop Makes respects your privacy.

Data Collection

Loop Makes does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, or developer-operated account systems.

Gameplay preferences and settings that need to be remembered by the app are stored locally on your device.

In-App Purchases

Loop Makes offers optional in-app purchases to unlock the full game.

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Loop Makes does not collect or have access to your payment card details or other payment information.

The app may receive purchase status and transaction-related information from Apple solely to verify purchases, enable purchased features, and support the restoration of purchases.

Purchase information is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy practices and terms.

Loop Makes does not independently operate a payment system or maintain a server-side database of purchase information.

Game Center

Loop Makes may use Apple Game Center for features such as leaderboards.

Game Center information is processed by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy practices. Loop Makes does not independently collect, store, or maintain your Game Center account information.

Any Game Center data made available through Apple's services is used only as necessary to provide Game Center features within the app.

Permissions

Loop Makes does not request access to sensitive device information or features such as your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Children's Privacy

Loop Makes does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because Loop Makes does not collect personal information, no personal user data is retained by the developer.

Third-Party Services

Loop Makes uses Apple services for platform functionality, including Game Center and App Store in-app purchases.

These services are provided and processed by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy practices.

Loop Makes does not use other third-party services that collect user information on behalf of the developer.

Data Retention and Deletion

Loop Makes does not maintain a server-side user database and does not retain personal information.

Local app preferences can be removed by deleting the app from your device.

Information associated with Apple Game Center and App Store purchases is managed by Apple and is subject to Apple's policies and account controls.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change.

If Loop Makes introduces functionality in the future that changes how user information is handled, this Privacy Policy and the corresponding App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.
